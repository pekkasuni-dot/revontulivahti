Webcams

Worldwide all-sky cameras and live aurora webcams near the auroral oval.

Submit a webcam

This page gives you an overview of webcams near the auroral oval at locations around the world. These webcams or all-sky cameras are great tools to see if the northern or southern lights are visible near your location without having to leave the house, or to just enjoy the aurora show going on at the other side of the world. Do you know of an all-sky camera that we might have missed? Let us know!
